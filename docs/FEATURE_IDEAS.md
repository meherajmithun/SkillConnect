# Feature Ideas for SkillConnect

SkillConnect already lets customers find professionals, request quotes, book
appointments, send messages, and leave reviews. These ideas build on those
features.

## Suggested features

### 1. Booking reminders and rescheduling

2

**Why it helps:** People are less likely to miss appointments, and they have a
clear way to handle a change of plans.

### 2. Faster message updates

Show new messages sooner in an open conversation. Today, the app checks for new
messages every few seconds.

**Why it helps:** Customers and professionals can coordinate work more easily.

### 3. Better travel distance estimates

Show the travel distance along roads between a customer and a professional.
The current distance is measured in a straight line on the map.

**Why it helps:** A road distance can give customers a more useful idea of how
far away a professional is.

### 4. More service areas

Add more towns and neighborhoods to the area list so professionals can choose
where they work.

**Why it helps:** Customers can find professionals who serve their area as the
marketplace grows.

### 5. Professional business reports

Show professionals how their business changes over time, such as how many
booking requests they receive and which services are most popular.

**Why it helps:** Professionals can understand their activity and decide what
to improve.

### 6. Problem reports and refunds

Give customers and professionals a way to report a problem with a service and
let staff review it. If SkillConnect later handles payment for services, add a
clear way to review refund requests too.

**Why it helps:** People need a fair process when something goes wrong. This is
a larger feature because SkillConnect does not currently collect payment for
the service itself or issue service refunds.

## Recommended order

1. **Booking reminders and rescheduling** is implemented. Both participants
   get in-app reminders before accepted appointments. Either participant can
   suggest a new time, and the other person can accept or decline it.
2. Finish **durable portfolio photo storage** so uploaded photos remain
   available after a server restart or update. See the
   [photo storage plan](DURABLE_PORTFOLIO_STORAGE_PLAN.md).
3. Improve message updates and travel distance after the booking flow is
   reliable.
4. Add more service areas and professional business reports as the number of
   users grows.
5. Plan problem reports and refunds as a separate, larger project.

## What the first feature includes

The booking reminder and rescheduling feature includes:

1. Remind both people about an upcoming booking.
2. Let a customer or professional suggest a new time.
3. Let the other person accept or decline the suggestion.
4. Update the booking and notify both people when a new time is accepted.
5. Keep a record of the agreed time so both people see the same schedule.

This order improves an important existing part of SkillConnect before adding
larger new services.
